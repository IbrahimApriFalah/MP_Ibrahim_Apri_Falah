## Editorial and asset policy

- Shirt numbers are shown as unavailable because the November training-camp announcement did not publish them.
- Individual career statistics are not invented or displayed as verified statistics.
- Only supplied image assets that clearly match a named player are used as individual portraits. Other players use a neutral silhouette graphic that is explicitly labelled as an unavailable portrait; a team/supporter photograph is not passed off as an individual player image.
- The homepage text is supporter-oriented editorial copy, not a news article or official PSSI publication. Factual statements about players, results, coach, and dates are tied to the sources above.
