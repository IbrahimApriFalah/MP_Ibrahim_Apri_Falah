01 / THE SPIRIT OF GARUDA

MORE THAN NINETY MINUTES.

OUR PURPOSE & CONNECTION

Where the terrace roar meets the badge on the chest.

Across islands, languages, and generations, the Indonesian National Football Team turns millions of individual hopes into a single collective heartbeat. Every matchday begins long before kickoff—in the chants, the red shirts, and the shared belief that Garuda belongs on the grandest stages.

Garuda Fans exists as a supporter-built digital archive: an editorial space to know the squad, track upcoming matchday status, and leave words of encouragement for the team.
